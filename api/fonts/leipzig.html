Oddities & Known Issues: Leipzig is currently experimental in Scribe. Several SMuFL glyphs—notably certain chord symbol extensions, accidental shifts, and noteheads—are either absent or possess idiosyncratic metrics. Use with mild caution and a sense of adventure.
